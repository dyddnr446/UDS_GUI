2. uds.io 패키지 (불변 record):

   - LogReader: UTF-8, 헤더 ts,direction,hex, #/빈줄 무시, 깨진 줄 LineError (FR-01, NFR-02, NFR-10)
3. uds.catalog.model + uds.catalog.load (PACKAGE_MAP):
   - model: ControllerProfile, DidDefinition, DidProfileSupport, SymptomDidMap,
     ServiceDefinition, SubFunctionDefinition, NrcDefinition,
     DtcStatusBitDefinition, LayoutDefinition
   - load.UdsCatalog: 불변 Map/List + catalogVersion=SHA-256 (FR-03, FR-20, FR-22)
   - load.CatalogLoader: POI로 docs/uds_catalog.xlsx 로드. Workbook/Sheet/Row/Cell 외부 노출 금지 (FR-22)
